What we do for estate libraries

We know what estate work looks like — often you're handling everything for a loved one who just passed, and the books feel too heavy to think about. That's what we're here for. We come to the house, work quietly, pack every book ourselves, and hand you a written confirmation before we leave.

Situations we handle

  • Loss of a family member — full house of books to clear
  • Downsizing to a smaller home or assisted living
  • Executor working through an estate with a book collection
  • Estate sale companies with unsold books after the sale
  • Property managers preparing a home for sale
  • Long-distance heirs who need someone reliable on the ground

How an estate pickup works

  1. Call or fill out the form. Tell us roughly how many books, the address, and any timing constraints.
  2. We confirm. A team member responds within 1 business day with a pickup window.
  3. We arrive with boxes and a vehicle. You don't need to pack anything.
  4. We work at your pace. Whether it's an hour or a full day, we stay until it's done.
  5. Written confirmation. Before we leave, you get a pickup confirmation for your records.

Estate library FAQ

Is there really no charge for an estate library cleanout?

Yes. We pick up estate libraries at no cost, regardless of the size. No fees, no fuel charges, no minimums.

Can you handle a full house of books?

Yes. We regularly handle entire home libraries and multi-room collections. For very large jobs we schedule a multi-day pickup or bring a second vehicle.

I'm the executor — do you provide any paperwork?

Yes. We give you a written pickup confirmation with the date, address, and estimated number of boxes. If you need something specific for probate or estate records, tell us in advance and we can adjust the format.

What happens to the books after pickup?

Every book is sorted by hand. Books that can be resold or passed along to a new reader are prepared for that. Books that cannot be reused are recycled responsibly.

Do you handle out-of-state family situations?

Yes. We regularly work with heirs who live outside California. We coordinate over phone and email, and can pick up when nobody is home if a neighbor or realtor grants access.

How far ahead should I schedule an estate pickup?

For flexible timelines, one week's notice is plenty. If you're on a deadline (property closing, out-of-town family visit), reach out and we'll do our best to accommodate.

Ready to schedule?

Free, local, and we handle everything. One-box minimum.
