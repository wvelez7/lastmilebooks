Last Mile Books

Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

Last Mile Books picks up donated books in Riverside County, California, and resells them online. This policy explains what information we handle and how we use it.

Book donation pickup requests

When you request a pickup, we collect your name, email address, phone number, pickup address, city, number of boxes, preferred date, and any notes you add.

We use this only to schedule and complete your pickup and to contact you about it. Requests are stored in our database and emailed to our team.

eBay and Whatnot accounts

We connect only our own seller accounts, to keep our inventory accurate across marketplaces and to price and manage our listings. Through that connection we use:

  • Listing details such as title, price, quantity, SKU, ISBN, condition, shipping cost, image, and link.
  • Order details needed to update stock: order and item IDs, title, SKU, and quantity sold.
  • Prices of other sellers' public listings for the same book, shown when we look a book up and not stored.
  • The access credentials that keep the connection working.

We do not store buyers' names, addresses, or payment details in this system. Buyer information needed to ship an order stays with the marketplace. We do not use eBay data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Who we share information with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with the service providers that run our website and email, Render (hosting) and Resend (email delivery), so they can provide those services, or when the law requires it.

Cookies and tracking

Our website does not use advertising or analytics trackers.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep pickup requests for as long as we need them to run our donation program and meet our legal obligations. Listing data is replaced as our inventory changes.

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. California residents have these rights under state privacy law. We also respond to eBay's account deletion notifications.

Security

Connections to our website are encrypted, and our inventory tools are password protected. No system is perfectly secure, so we limit what we collect.

Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new effective date.
