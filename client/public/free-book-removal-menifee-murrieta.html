How it works

  1. Tell us roughly how many books. A guess is fine, and a photo helps us bring enough boxes.
  2. Pick a Tuesday or Wednesday. We confirm your pickup window within one business day.
  3. We pack and carry it out. We arrive with boxes, pack the books where they are, and load everything up.
  4. You get the room back. Plus a written pickup confirmation for your records.

Who calls us

  • Downsizing or getting ready to move
  • Turning a spare room or office back into living space
  • Clearing out a garage, closet or storage unit
  • Handling a parent's or grandparent's library
  • Readers and collectors ready to let go

What we take

Almost every kind of book: hardcovers, paperbacks, fiction, non-fiction, cookbooks, children's books, textbooks, art books, religious books and whole personal libraries. The only books we can't take are encyclopedia sets and books that are wet, moldy or falling apart.

Every book is sorted by hand. We resell or pass along what we can so it reaches a new reader, and anything that can't be reused is recycled.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no charge for the pickup, the boxes, the packing or the carrying.

Do I need to box anything?

No. On the form, choose "Still on the shelves" and we'll bring boxes and pack everything ourselves. If you've already boxed or bagged the books, just set them out by the door.

How many books do you need?

At least a box's worth, with no upper limit. We've cleared whole home libraries.

When do you pick up?

Tuesdays and Wednesdays. Pick the day that works for you and we'll confirm a time window within one business day.

Do you come outside Menifee and Murrieta?

Yes. We also pick up in Temecula, Lake Elsinore, Wildomar, French Valley and Winchester.
